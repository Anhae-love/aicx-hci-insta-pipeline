**Topic** (Tuesday: chatbots, anthropomorphism, emotion, social interaction): **"비서 이름을 바꾼 사람들의 말투"**
- **Source:** arXiv 2610.13156, a preprint under review for CHI 2027. I read the full text, and all 25 quotes in `facts.json` match it word for word.
- **Study:** three months of real chat logs from one desktop AI assistant, 257 users.
- **Renaming:** 118 of them (45.9%) changed the assistant's default name.
- **Openings:** among the 103 users with at least 3 questions, those who renamed it tended to open with greetings more; those who kept the default used more commands.
- **Revised assistant:** the researchers built new replies that greet back and acknowledge feelings first. In 60.1% of the renamers' judgments, they picked the new reply as treating them "as someone to talk to". For default-name users it was 38.0%.
