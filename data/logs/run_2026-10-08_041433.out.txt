**Topic (Thursday: recommendation systems and algorithms):** *Unintended Consequences of Recommender System Interventions* (arXiv 2606.08265, a preprint).
- **The experiment:** a large Chinese short-video platform randomly picked some users. For them, it raised the recommendation score of celebrity "time to go to sleep" videos between 1 and 5 a.m. The analysis covered 200,000 users.
- **The result:** late-night app use in that group went up 14.75% during the campaign week, the opposite of the intent. The increase was still 3.6–6.9% eight weeks after the campaign ended.
- **The authors' explanation:** the recommendation algorithm learned from how people reacted to those videos. This is their interpretation, not something they observed directly.
