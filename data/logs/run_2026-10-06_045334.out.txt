**Topic:** an experiment inside a public Santa hotline in Germany ([arXiv 2609.38782](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38782)). Children's calls were randomly routed to one of four AI voice agents. The agent called itself either Santa or "Santa's Helper", and either just chatted or gently asked about wishes for someone else.
- When the agent was set up to ask, 46% of calls included a wish for someone else, against 12% when it just chatted.
- Santa versus Helper made no clear difference to that rate.
- Calls the Helper answered were much more often over within a minute: 65% against 39% for Santa, counted over all 494 calls in the study window.
